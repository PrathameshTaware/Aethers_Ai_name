SAMPLE CIRCULAR, WRITTEN FOR THE SAANS DEMO. NOT A REAL GOVERNMENT ORDER.

DIRECTORATE OF EDUCATION (SAMPLE)

Sub: Directions under Stage-IV of Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP).

Consequent upon the invocation of Stage-IV ('Severe+' Air Quality) of GRAP in NCR, all Heads of Schools are directed as under:

1. Classes VI to IX and Class XI shall run in hybrid mode, with online classes wherever feasible.
2. Outdoor sports for students of Classes X and XII preparing for board practicals may be held in the afternoon.
3. Morning assemblies shall not be held until further orders.
4. All outdoor sports and physical education activities in schools shall remain suspended under Stage IV of GRAP.

This issues with the approval of the Competent Authority.
